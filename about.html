Hi, I'm Elisa Stella.

For as long as I can remember, I've been the one who checks in, sends the note, and makes sure someone has a warm meal and a listening ear. I was raised with a lot of love, and a grandmother who showed me what steady, unconditional care looks like. Since my early twenties I've found myself stepping in as a "mom" for people who didn't have one nearby.

In 2025 I turned that calling into Everyone Needs a Mom, for anyone who has lost a mother's love through loss, distance, or a broken relationship, and for anyone who simply feels alone.

Our video sessions are a chance to sit down together, face to face. Tell me what's on your heart, celebrate your wins, talk through the hard days, and leave knowing someone is in your corner.

Things we can talk about

  • Everyday life & growing up
  • Work & school
  • Family & relationships
  • Loneliness
  • Big decisions
  • Good news to celebrate!
