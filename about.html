About

Hi, I’m Shira.

I’m a mom, and I live in sunny Florida. I know how much it means to have someone in your corner: someone who checks in, really listens, and cheers for every win, big or small.

That’s what I’d love to be for you. Whether you’re missing a mother’s love because of loss, distance, or a broken relationship, or you’re simply going through a lonely stretch, you don’t have to go through it alone.

Our calls are a chance to sit down together, by video or by phone. Tell me what’s on your heart, celebrate your wins, talk through the hard days, and leave knowing someone is in your corner.

Things we can talk about

  • Everyday life & growing up
  • Work & school
  • Family & relationships
  • Loneliness
  • Big decisions
  • Good news to celebrate
