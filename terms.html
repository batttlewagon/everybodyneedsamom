05Not for emergencies

Calls are not for crises or emergencies. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, U.S.) or call 911.

06Privacy and respect

  • Calls are not recorded. Please do not record a call without written permission.

Fees are paid when your call is confirmed. You can reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your call for a full refund. Later cancellations and missed calls are not refunded.
